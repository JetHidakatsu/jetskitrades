# Trading Bot Management Documentation

## Overview
This directory contains management-focused documentation for tracking the development, legal compliance, and financial aspects of our Solana trading bot. These documents are designed to be accessible and useful for non-technical stakeholders while maintaining alignment with technical development.

## Document Structure

### 1. Development Stages (`development_stages.md`)
- Comprehensive breakdown of development phases
- Task tracking templates
- Progress monitoring guidelines
- Success criteria for each stage

### 2. Legal Requirements (`legal_checklist.md`)
- Regulatory compliance checklist
- Risk management protocols
- Documentation requirements
- Review schedules
- Legal task templates

### 3. Financial Oversight (`financial_oversight.md`)
- Budget planning and tracking
- Performance metrics
- ROI analysis templates
- Financial review schedules
- Cost management guidelines

## How to Use These Documents

### For Legal Team
1. Use `legal_checklist.md` to:
   - Track compliance requirements
   - Monitor risk management
   - Schedule regular reviews
   - Document legal considerations

### For Financial Team
1. Use `financial_oversight.md` to:
   - Monitor development costs
   - Track performance metrics
   - Analyze ROI
   - Plan budgets
   - Schedule financial reviews

### For Project Management
1. Use `development_stages.md` to:
   - Track development progress
   - Assign and monitor tasks
   - Ensure alignment between technical and business goals
   - Plan resource allocation

## Best Practices

1. **Regular Updates**
   - Review and update documents weekly
   - Mark completed items
   - Add new requirements as identified

2. **Cross-Reference**
   - Link related items across documents
   - Ensure consistency in reporting
   - Maintain clear communication channels

3. **Documentation**
   - Keep all entries dated
   - Include responsible parties
   - Document decisions and their rationale

## Task Creation Guidelines

1. **Clear Objectives**
   - Define specific goals
   - Set measurable outcomes
   - Establish clear timelines

2. **Proper Assignment**
   - Identify responsible parties
   - Set realistic deadlines
   - Include necessary resources

3. **Progress Tracking**
   - Regular status updates
   - Clear success criteria
   - Documented completion
